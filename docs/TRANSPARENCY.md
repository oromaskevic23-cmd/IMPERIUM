# Transparency, Market Integrity and Risks
**Policy draft v0.1. Not a prospectus or a regulatory crypto-asset white paper.**

## Public evidence standard
Every objective claim must include its date, primary evidence and material limitations. Distinguish *implemented*, *tested*, *planned*, *blocked* and *independently verified*. Correct material inaccuracies promptly. Avoid invented holders, pricing, trading volume, wallets, launch claims, audits or partners.

## Risks
- **Financial:** highly volatile price, possible complete loss, slippage, insufficient buyers or liquidity.
- **Technical:** smart-contract, RPC, chain, custody, operational and signer failure.
- **Concentration:** token ownership and unlocks may materially affect markets; concentration has not yet been independently evaluated here.
- **Regulatory:** jurisdiction-specific token classification, financial promotions, KYC/AML, public offers and trading admission rules.
- **Commercial:** no guarantee AI platforms, users or enterprises will use IMPERIUM.
- **Governance:** corporate ownership and contractual holder rights require independently validated legal documentation.

## Communications controls
No guaranteed profits, target-price assurances, false exchange rumors, synthetic testimonials, fake scarcity, wash trading or coordinated price manipulation. Disclose paid communications where applicable. Public AI-agent demonstrations must clearly be labeled test/demo where applicable. Token usage should be tied to real service orders, not artificial on-chain churn.

## Status
A verified contract exists. Global AI-agent monetary adoption and exchange approval are long-term goals, not current established facts.
