# Exchange Listing Readiness
**Pre-application preparation only. No submission, approval or listing is claimed.**

## Official application channels
- [Coinbase listings](https://www.coinbase.com/listings): review request followed by discretionary screening and due diligence. Coinbase describes support for Polygon ERC-20 assets; support does not imply eligibility for any particular token.
- [Kraken listing application](https://www.kraken.com/get-listed): review of identity, technology, market quality, security and legal posture. Kraken explicitly states no listing fee or paid fast-track.
- [EU ESMA MiCA overview](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica).
- [MiCA Article 6 white-paper content](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-6-content-and-form-crypto-asset).

## Required dossier
1. Real issuer/offeror identity, applicant authority, governance, legal entities and KYC.
2. Exact chain identity, source code, contract controls, independent audit and deployment records.
3. Supply and decimals; circulating figures and insider/treasury wallets; allocation, vesting, unlock schedules and distribution methodology.
4. Trading venues, order books/pools, on-chain volume and actual liquidity — supported by dated independent data.
5. Demonstrated product utility, deployment evidence, users and externally reproducible activity.
6. Security incident policy, custody controls, key management, monitoring and known risks.
7. Jurisdiction-specific legal classification, counsel-reviewed disclosures and applicable white paper and notification procedures.
8. Conflict disclosures, communications policy, eligible trading regions, contact and public investor risk disclosure.

## Listing gate
```text
Contract verification -> supply/rights attestation -> security assessment
-> truthful utility evidence -> legal review -> exchange dossier
-> direct official exchange application -> independent exchange decision
```

Never announce a listing, endorsement, launch date or partnership until independently confirmed by the relevant exchange. Never use wash trading, fake volume, undisclosed influencer sponsorship, coordinated price pumping or fabricated market-maker endorsements.
