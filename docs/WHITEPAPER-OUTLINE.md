# White Paper — Preparation Outline
**This checklist is NOT the white paper and is NOT regulatory authorization.**

A qualified legal reviewer must determine requirements in each target jurisdiction before a public offer or exchange admission. For EU-facing activities, see [MiCA Article 6](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-6-content-and-form-crypto-asset) and the [ESMA MiCA overview](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica).

Required research: accountable offeror/issuer; asset identity and rights; detailed project and technology; actual current product status; contract privileges; independently attested supply, allocations and liquidity; token-holder obligations; admission details; governance; foreseeable technology, market and regulatory risks; applicable environmental disclosures; version control and formal legal sign-off.

**Missing evidence:** live supply and allocation attestation, independent audit, issuer legal assessment, market/liquidity proof and legal review. Do not present this document as MiCA-compliant.
